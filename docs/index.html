In plain English

What is this, exactly?

You don't need to know anything about options to follow along.

🏠

What's an option?

Like paying a small deposit to hold a price. Pay $5k for the right to buy a $500k house at that price for 3 months. If prices jump to $600k, your deal is worth a lot. If they fall, you walk away and lose only the $5k. Stock options work the same way.

CALL · bet it goes upPUT · bet it goes down
📊

What does the app do?

Thousands of options trade on every big company, at different prices and dates. That's a wall of numbers. The app pulls it for free and turns it into charts and plain-English summaries: what traders expect, how nervous they are, and where the big bets sit. You can test a trade before risking real money.

🧺

What's the Portfolio tab?

A health check for your investments. Owning 10 things feels safe, but if they're mostly tech, or funds that hold the same big companies, one bad week hits almost everything. It shows where your money really sits and how risky the mix is.

Features

Everything a trading desk looks at.

Every number is computed in your browser from live (15-minute delayed) market data.

01 · OVERVIEW

Plain-English reading

Expected move, volatility vs. history, skew, positioning and liquidity, written out in sentences.

02 · PRICE

Candles + implied range

Price history with moving averages and the range the options market expects for each expiry.

03 · CHAIN

The full option chain

Calls and puts by strike with IV and Greeks. Click any contract for an explanation and what-if sliders.

04 · VOLATILITY

Skew, term structure, 3D

Volatility across strikes and expiries, plus a 3D surface you can drag to turn and scroll to zoom.

05 · OPEN INTEREST

Where the bets are

Open interest and volume by strike, max pain and estimated dealer gamma.

06 · STRATEGY

Multi-leg builder

14 templates with payoff chart, break-evens, chance of profit and net Greeks.

07 · CALCULATOR

Price any option

Black-Scholes and binomial models, implied-volatility solver, first- and second-order Greeks.

08 · LEARN

13 interactive lessons

The Greeks taught with live examples, sliders and quizzes.

09 · PORTFOLIO

Your holdings, x-rayed

Value, splits by sector and country with funds opened up, and a diversification check.

Tour

Nine views, one keystroke each.

Type a ticker, then press 1–9. Every screen is a bookmarkable link.

Portfolio

Are your eggs really in different baskets?

  • 1
    Bring your holdingsA Wealthsimple report, any CSV, typed in by hand, or an optional read-only brokerage link.
  • 2
    See where the money really isBy sector, asset type, currency and country, with funds opened up into what they hold.
  • 3
    Check the riskConcentration, yearly swing, correlations, beta to the S&P 500 and TSX, and the deepest fall.

Your holdings file is read in your browser and never uploaded. Only ticker symbols leave it, to fetch prices. The app never buys or sells anything.

Get started

Running in under a minute.

All you need is uv. It fetches Python and the one library on first run.

STEP 1

Clone it

$ git clone https://github.com/Pseudocoder28/options-viewer.git
STEP 2

Run it

$ cd options-viewer $ uv run server.py

Opens http://127.0.0.1:8765 in your browser.

STEP 3 · OPTIONAL

Make it a Mac app

$ python3 make_app.py

Builds a double-click Options Terminal.app for your Dock.

Options Terminal is a learning and analysis tool. Data is delayed and unofficial, nothing here is investment advice, and the app cannot place orders.
